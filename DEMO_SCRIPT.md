# Stillway Demonstration Script

Target runtime: **2:15**. Hard stop: **2:30**.

## 0:00–0:20 — Brand and search

“Stillway is a premium sample flight marketplace built around one idea: travel at your rhythm. The home screen keeps every core choice visible—round trip, route, dates, and travelers—with Atlanta to San Francisco prefilled from our SQLite inventory.”

- Show the cinematic hero and overlapping search panel.
- Point out “Demo experience · no payment” and the sample-data trust language.
- Select **Search flights**; results remain on the same page.

## 0:20–0:55 — Results and Journey Fit

“The custom feature is Journey Fit. Price never disappears, but the same flights can be ranked around how I want to arrive.”

- Show the Balanced order and visible price, time, stops, emissions, and explanation.
- Select **Value** and show the lower fare move to the top.
- Select **Arrive rested** and show the nonstop daytime option return to the top.
- Open **Fine-tune** and move one slider.
- Explain that all weights normalize automatically and every score names its strongest contributors.

## 0:55–1:25 — Assemble the round trip

“Stillway asks for one decision at a time.”

- Choose the outbound.
- Choose the return.
- Point to the persistent itinerary, locked sample total, traveler count, and no-payment line.
- Continue to guest details.

## 1:25–1:55 — Guest checkout and confirmation

“This is a guest booking, so there is no account and no payment form.”

- Enter the demo traveler details.
- Show the seat preference and the explicit no-charge notice.
- Confirm the booking.
- Point out the human-readable confirmation code, separate refresh-safe URL, two stored legs, and confirmed total.

## 1:55–2:15 — Persistence proof

“The confirmation is not a hard-coded success screen. It is stored locally in SQLite.”

- Refresh the confirmation page.
- Open **My Trips**.
- Enter the code and matching email.
- Show the recovered itinerary and close on “Travel at your rhythm.”

## Recording checklist

- Start the production server, not the development server.
- Use a 16:10 or 16:9 desktop viewport at 1080p or higher.
- Hide unrelated windows, notifications, and personal information.
- Use clearly fictional demo contact details.
- Keep pointer movement deliberate and leave a short beat after every state change.
- Verify the exported file is shorter than 2:30 before uploading.
